My story

When I was little I didn't just play pretend. I made the props, designed the costumes and gave every game its own identity.

I didn't know it then, but I was already a designer.

I grew up in South Texas. My Mexican heritage, my Catholic upbringing and the streets of downtown Corpus Christi show up in almost everything I make. So do the subjects I keep coming back to: identity, memory, belonging, grief and faith.

A project usually starts with a feeling or a memory I can't put into words yet. I research and collect images, references, textures and words. Then I experiment, by hand and on screen, until the type, image, composition and colour start speaking the same language.

I'm drawn to the tension between order and chaos. I want work that's expressive and layered but still does its job. Whether it's a publication, an identity or a print, I'm looking for the balance between what it needs to say and what I want you to feel.

How I work

From a feeling to a system.

  1. 01

    Research

    I collect imagery, references, textures and words until something feels connected to the idea.

  2. 02

    Experiment

    Sketches, collage and marker comps, by hand and on screen.

  3. 03

    Build a language

    Type, image-making, composition and colour turn the idea into a visual system.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Critique and refinement, round after round, then mockups and print tests.
