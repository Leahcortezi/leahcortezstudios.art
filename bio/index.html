My story

When I was little, I didn't just play pretend. I made the props, designed the costumes, and gave every game its own identity.

I didn't know it then, but I was already a designer.

I grew up in South Texas, and my Mexican heritage, Catholic upbringing, and surroundings in Corpus Christi have influenced how I see the world and approach my work. I'm interested in identity, memory, culture, and the ways visual communication can tell a story.

As a designer, I enjoy taking an idea and figuring out how to communicate it visually. I start with research and references, then experiment with typography, imagery, color, and composition until the pieces work together. I like exploring different directions and finding solutions that are both visually interesting and purposeful.

I'm drawn to expressive design, strong typography, unusual imagery, and the balance between structure and experimentation. Whether I'm designing a publication, developing an identity, or creating a poster, I want the final work to have a clear concept and a point of view.

How I work

From a feeling to a system.

  1. 01

    Research

    I collect imagery, references, textures and words until something feels connected to the idea.

  2. 02

    Experiment

    Sketches, collage and marker comps, by hand and on screen.

  3. 03

    Build a language

    Type, image-making, composition and colour turn the idea into a visual system.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Critique and refinement, round after round, then mockups and print tests.
