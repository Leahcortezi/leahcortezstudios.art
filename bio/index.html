About

I’m Leah Cortez, a multidisciplinary designer specializing in publication, identity, and print-led systems. My work blends strategic typographic systems with expressive imagery to help cultural and editorial projects communicate clearly while retaining a distinct visual voice. I collaborate with small presses, creative directors, and brands to develop concepts, visual systems, and production-ready assets.

Core specialties: art direction, editorial design, type system development, cover and packaging design, and print production. Primary tools: Adobe InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop, and production workflows for print and digital delivery.

What I Offer

- Publication design (covers, interiors, full-wrap layouts)
- Brand & identity systems
- Type & specimen development
- Print-ready file preparation and vendor coordination

For hire: contract work, short-term projects, and ongoing retainer relationships for design and art direction. Available for remote and on-site projects by request.
