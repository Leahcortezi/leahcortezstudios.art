City Reliquary

A visual identity for a community museum celebrating everyday artifacts, featuring a negative-space CR mark and a system of promotional and environmental applications.

Role
Graphic Designer
Year
2026
Deliverables
CR logo mark, brand identity system, posters, signage

Overview

Project

City Reliquary is a conceptual brand identity for a community museum in New York City dedicated to everyday artifacts and the stories they preserve. The identity explores how ordinary objects can become meaningful records of personal memory, neighborhood history, and shared culture.

Creative direction

The visual identity centers on a CR mark that uses negative space to create a distinctive, compact symbol. The mark provides a recognizable foundation for a broader identity system intended to work across promotional materials and environmental applications, reflecting the museum’s interest in preserving objects that might otherwise be overlooked.

Design approach

The identity balances recognition with flexibility. The core mark is supported by applications that show how the system can operate across different formats while maintaining a consistent visual language. Posters, signage, and promotional materials extend the identity beyond the logo and into public-facing contexts.
