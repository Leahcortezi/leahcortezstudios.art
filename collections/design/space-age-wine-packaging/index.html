Space Age

A rosé label with an astronaut, and a vineyard reflected in the visor.

Deliverables
Front and back labels, astronaut illustration, bottle capsule, prototypes
01 — The storySpace Age
The brief

Bring something unexpected to wine packaging without losing the product inside the concept.

The idea

An astronaut as the focal point, with a vineyard reflected in the visor to tie outer space back to where wine comes from.

The result

A label system across the front, back and capsule, tested as printed prototypes and bottle mockups.
