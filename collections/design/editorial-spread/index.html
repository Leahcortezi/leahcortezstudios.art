Alien Invasion or Sleep Paralysis?

Spreads about the uneasy space between dreaming and waking, with distorted type, black-and-white imagery and a strict grid.

Deliverables
Editorial spreads, display type, black-and-white imagery
01 — The storyAlien Invasion or Sleep Paralysis?
The brief

Make a half-asleep, liminal experience feel uneasy on the page without making the text hard to read.

The idea

Distorted display type for the instability, a structured text grid for clarity, and monochrome imagery for the atmosphere.

The result

Spreads that hold tension and order at once, like being caught between a dream and waking up.

"The topic fascinated me, and it gave me a chance to explore the visual confusion between two surreal experiences."
