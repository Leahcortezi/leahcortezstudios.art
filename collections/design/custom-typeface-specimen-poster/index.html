Case study · Typography · 2026

Custom Typeface + Specimen Poster

The goal of this project was to translate a historical metal or wood type specimen into a functional contemporary digital typeface.

Role
Typeface and poster design
Tools
Illustrator, InDesign
Year
2026
Deliverables
Typeface + specimen

Overview

Challenge

The challenge was to preserve the character and visual identity of the original reference while adapting it for modern use, with a strong focus on accessibility, readability, and consistency across a full character set.

Solution

I translated the character of historical wood and metal type into Surendra, a contemporary digital typeface with consistent stroke weight, open counters, and carefully balanced spacing.

Outcome

The completed glyph set and specimen poster demonstrate how a historically informed display face can retain expressive character while remaining functional and legible.

01 — Sketches

From historical specimen to digital type

I studied the original letterforms, traced their defining features, and refined the digital characters through repeated spacing and kerning checks. The goal was to preserve the source’s personality while making the full character set feel consistent.
