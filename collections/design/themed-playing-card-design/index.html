Scientific Revolution Deck

A playing card deck celebrating the Scientific Revolution, with Darwin, Lavoisier, Newton and Tesla as the kings.

Deliverables

01 — The storyScientific Revolution Deck
The brief

Merge historical accuracy with engaging visual design, turning educational content into a real deck that respects these legacies and still plays like normal cards.

The idea

Each suit is a field (biology, chemistry, physics and technology), with one of its pioneers as the king.
