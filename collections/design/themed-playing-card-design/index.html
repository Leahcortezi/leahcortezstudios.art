Scientific Revolution Playing Cards

A Scientific Revolution playing-card deck featuring pioneering figures across Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Technology.

Role
Illustration and playing-card design
Tools
Illustrator, Photoshop
Year
2024
Deliverables
Assets & print files

Overview

Challenge

The challenge was to merge historical accuracy with engaging visual design—transforming educational content into a tangible artifact that respects intellectual legacy while maintaining the playful structure of traditional playing cards.

Solution

I designed a themed deck that organizes influential figures across Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Technology within a familiar playing-card system.

Outcome

The final cards pair researched historical figures with a consistent visual system, turning educational content into a collectible, playable format.
