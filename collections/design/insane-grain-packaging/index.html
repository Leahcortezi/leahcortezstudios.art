Insane Grain

A rebrand for Insane Grain, a sorghum-based health snack whose packaging was far quieter than its name. The new pouch is built to grab younger, trend-conscious snackers without losing the health story.

Role
Brand & packaging design
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator
Deliverables
Pouch, Strikin' Hot flavor, graphic system, mockups
01 — The storyInsane Grain
The brief

The old packaging didn't carry the energy or boldness the name promised, and it wasn't speaking to the younger, trend-conscious audience the brand wanted.

The idea

A loud collage system: a halftone mouth, real chilies and inky flame linework, locked into hard red, black and white blocks with tall condensed type.

The result

A pouch that sells heat first. The health claims (sorghum, gut health, 47% less fat) get their own black band, so they still read at a glance.

02 — ResearchInsane Grain

Borrowing from collage posters

I pulled collage portraits, halftone photography and loud poster typography: work that feels cut up, layered and a little unhinged, which is exactly what the name asks for.

03 — SketchesInsane Grain

Two early directions

A digital collage tested how photography, halftone and flat colour could share one surface. A marker sketch blocked out the grid underneath it.
