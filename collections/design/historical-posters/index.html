Historical Posters

A collection of poster designs inspired by historical design movements and visual traditions.

Role
Research and poster design
Tools

Year
2026
Deliverables
Poster designs

Overview

Challenge

A collection of poster designs inspired by historical design movements and visual traditions.

Solution

Each poster translates a researched design movement into a contemporary composition, moving between strict grids, dynamic geometry, collage, and minimal symbolism.

Outcome

The nine-week series resulted in nine finished posters and written reflections, with Dada, the Stenberg Brothers, and Modernism among the strongest directions.

02 — Sketches

From thumbnail to final poster

I developed thumbnail compositions before moving into digital drafts, testing hierarchy, contrast, and movement. The final series preserves the character of each movement while making every poster work as a distinct contemporary piece.

03 — Concept development

Translating history into contemporary work

Rather than applying one style across the entire series, I adapted the visual logic of each movement to the subject. The changing compositions show how research can guide design decisions while still leaving room for interpretation.
