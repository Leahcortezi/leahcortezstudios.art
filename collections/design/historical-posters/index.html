Historical Poster Styles

Nine weeks, nine posters: Dada, the Stenberg brothers, Modernism and more.

Deliverables

01 — The storyHistorical Poster Styles
The brief

Study historical design movements as systems to understand, not styles to copy.

The idea

Research and thumbnails first (25 for some movements), then refined compositions that reinterpret each reference.

The result

A nine-poster series. Dada, the Stenberg brothers and Modernism are the strongest directions.
