Designer Tribute Zine

An eight-page folded zine and a quote poster celebrating designer Jonathan Castro.

Deliverables
Eight-page folded zine, quote poster
01 — The storyDesigner Tribute Zine
The brief

Celebrate Jonathan Castro's way of working: emotional energy, layered references and a lot of experimenting.

The idea

Layered, expressive spreads that unfold across eight folded pages, plus a poster that boils the idea down to one statement.

The result

Two related pieces: a compact zine that builds page by page, and a poster about design, process and meaning piling up.

"I was drawn to his vibrant colors, chaotic compositions, and the way he draws inspiration from his Latin culture. That is something I deeply connect with."
