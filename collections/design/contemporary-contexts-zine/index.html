Case study · Editorial · 2025

Designer Tribute Zine + Poster

An eight-page tribute to designer Jonathan Castro, translating his layered visual energy into a zine and reverse-side quote poster.

Role
Editorial and poster design
Tools
InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop
Year
2025
Deliverables
Zine + poster

Overview

Challenge

A project researching a contemporary artist and designing a publication inspired by their style. I chose Jonathan Castro for his vibrant colors, chaotic compositions, and the way he draws inspiration from his Latin culture.

Solution

I built an image-driven visual system with layered textures, distortion, repetition, and shifts in scale to echo Castro’s energetic approach while retaining my own authorship.

Outcome

The final folded zine prioritizes rhythm and immersion over conventional hierarchy, with a quote poster on the reverse: “Design is the accumulation of energy in an object.”

01 — Research

Studying Jonathan Castro’s visual language

I chose Jonathan Castro because I was drawn to his vibrant colors, chaotic compositions, and the way he draws inspiration from his Latin culture—something I deeply connect with as well.

02 — Sketches

Finding the right kind of chaos

The early layouts tested how far the imagery and type could be pushed while keeping the publication readable. I explored layered compositions, changes in scale, and repeated elements, then refined the pacing across the folded eight-page format.

03 — Concept development

From research to an immersive zine

Rather than copying individual visuals, I translated the energy behind Castro’s work into a system of image manipulation, texture, repetition, and contrast. The poster extends the publication’s idea outward, using a concise quote as its central message.
