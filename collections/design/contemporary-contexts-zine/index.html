Case study · Editorial · 2025

Designer Tribute Zine and Poster

An experimental designer tribute combining an eight-page folded zine and quote poster to explore emotional energy, visual layering, and creative process.

Role
Graphic Designer
Year
2025
Deliverables
Eight-page folded zine, quote poster

Overview

Project

This project celebrates designer Jonathan Castro through an experimental editorial zine and accompanying poster. The work responds to an approach to design centered on emotional energy, layered visual references, and process-driven experimentation, interpreting a creative philosophy through visual form rather than presenting biographical information.

Creative direction

Layering, expressive composition, and experimental visual treatments create an energetic editorial language. The zine format lets the work unfold across multiple pages, while the poster distills the central idea into a single statement. The poster features the quote: “Design is the accumulation of energy in an object.”

Design approach

The eight-page folded zine uses sequencing and visual variation to build a compact editorial experience. The poster creates a separate but related expression of the tribute, emphasizing the relationship between design, process, and accumulated meaning.
