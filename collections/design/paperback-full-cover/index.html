Paperback Full Cover

Publication Design & Book Packaging

Publication Design 2026

Concept

The objective of this project was to design a complete paperback cover that communicates the book's narrative before it is opened. The cover needed to function as a continuous visual composition across the front cover, spine, and back while establishing a clear visual identity.

Design Approach

I approached the design as a single uninterrupted canvas rather than three separate panels. Typography, imagery, and composition were carefully arranged to create continuity across the wrap, while hierarchy and spacing ensured the title and supporting information remained clear. Production requirements such as spine width, bleeds, and alignment were incorporated throughout the design process.

Result

The finished cover successfully integrates narrative, typography, and print production into one cohesive system. It demonstrates an understanding of publication design, production constraints, and how visual storytelling extends beyond a single cover image.
