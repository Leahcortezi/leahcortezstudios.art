Gulliver’s Travels — Paperback Cover

A satirical paperback cover for Jonathan Swift’s Gulliver’s Travels, using a conceptual rooster-and-egg illustration to interpret the novel’s absurd political conflict.

Role
Graphic Designer
Year
2026
Deliverables
Front cover, spine, back cover, conceptual illustration

Overview

Project

This paperback cover redesign interprets Jonathan Swift’s Gulliver’s Travels through the satirical conflict between the Big-Endians and Little-Endians. The design draws from the novel’s absurd dispute over which end of an egg should be broken, using the conflict as a visual metaphor for the disproportionate nature of ideological division.

Creative direction

The central illustration features a rooster crushing eggs, transforming a seemingly trivial disagreement into a dramatic image of destruction. The concept reflects Swift’s use of satire to expose the irrationality of political and cultural conflict, communicated through illustration and symbolism rather than a literal depiction of characters.

Design approach

The illustration anchors a complete paperback cover system. Typography, image placement, and the relationship between the front cover, spine, and back cover are treated as parts of one continuous design, connecting the novel’s historical themes with a clear contemporary visual interpretation.
