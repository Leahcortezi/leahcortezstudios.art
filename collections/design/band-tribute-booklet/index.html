Band Tribute Booklet

A typographic editorial booklet celebrating the visual identity of a selected band. The project focused on creating a cohesive publication system that translated the band's music and personality into print through hierarchy, pacing, imagery, and expressive typography.

Role
Editorial design
Tools
InDesign
Year
2026
Deliverables
16-page booklet

Overview

Challenge

Rather than simply documenting the band, the goal was to balance readability with a strong visual atmosphere while maintaining consistency across every spread.

Solution

I built a restrained editorial system around cream, charcoal, and burgundy, pairing serif typography with archival imagery, handwritten textures, and a clear grid.

Outcome

The 16-page booklet celebrates Billy Joel’s introspective songwriting through a cinematic sequence of spreads, lyrics, discography, and archival references rather than a conventional commercial portrait.
