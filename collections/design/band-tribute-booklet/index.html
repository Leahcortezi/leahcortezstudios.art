Band Tribute Booklet

Editorial Design & Music Branding

Editorial Design 2026

Concept

The goal of this project was to design an editorial system that doesn't simply document the band, but captures their personality through the formal language of typography, composition, and pacing. Rather than presenting information linearly, the booklet creates a visual rhythm that mirrors the energy and emotional arc of their music—each spread builds momentum, allowing readers to experience the band's work as a cohesive narrative.

The challenge was to balance accessibility with visual interest. The booklet needed to remain readable while feeling dynamic, ensuring that the design choices actively support and enhance the band's identity rather than compete with it.

Design Approach

I developed a structured grid-based system that provided consistency across spreads while allowing room for variation. The grid served as the foundation—strict enough to maintain visual coherence, flexible enough to accommodate different image sizes, text hierarchies, and compositional approaches from spread to spread.

Typography was selected and refined to reflect the band's aesthetic. Rather than applying one typeface uniformly, I used scale, weight, and spacing strategically to create visual rhythm. Leading and line length were calibrated for readability, but also to establish pacing—how long the eye stays on a page, and how quickly it moves to the next.

Image placement and layering became a primary design tool. Photographs and illustrations were sequenced to build visual momentum across spreads. I used overlapping, cropping, and varied scales to create depth and guide the eye through a deliberate narrative sequence. Color was introduced selectively to draw focus and establish visual hierarchy without overwhelming the composition.

Result

The finished booklet demonstrates how editorial systems communicate through form as much as content. The design choices—grid structure, type selection, image sequencing, spatial relationships—work together to create an experience that extends beyond the individual pages into a complete immersive narrative.

By treating the booklet as a designed system rather than a collection of layouts, each element reinforces the band's identity while maintaining visual clarity. The result proves that thoughtful editorial design can function as both a functional information system and an artistic statement in its own right.
