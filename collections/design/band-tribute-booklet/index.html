Band Tribute Booklet

Editorial Design & Music Branding

Editorial Design 2026

Concept

This project explores how editorial design can visually translate music into print. Rather than simply presenting information, the booklet was designed to capture the band's personality through typography, composition, and pacing, creating an experience that reflects the energy and atmosphere of their music.

Design Approach

I developed a structured editorial system using expressive typography, layered imagery, and a consistent grid to establish rhythm across each spread. Scale, hierarchy, and negative space were used to guide the reader while allowing individual layouts to feel dynamic. Every design decision—from typography to image placement—worked together to create a publication that balances readability with visual impact.

Result

The final booklet demonstrates my ability to build cohesive editorial systems that communicate through both content and form. By combining thoughtful typography with strong visual sequencing, the publication transforms printed pages into an immersive reading experience.
