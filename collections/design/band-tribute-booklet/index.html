Billy Joel Tribute Booklet

Sixteen pages on Billy Joel's songwriting, especially Vienna, in cream, charcoal and burgundy.

Deliverables
16-page booklet, typography and layout, colour system
01 — The storyBilly Joel Tribute Booklet
The brief

Capture the reflective feel of Billy Joel's songwriting, especially Vienna, in print.

The idea

A late-1970s palette of cream, charcoal and burgundy, archival-feeling references, and a structure that gives the music room to breathe.

The result

A 16-page booklet that works as both a tribute and an editorial reading of the songs.
