Band Tribute Booklet

Editorial Design & Music Branding

Editorial Design 2026

Concept

This project explores how editorial design can visually translate music into print. Rather than simply presenting information, the booklet was designed to capture the band's personality through typography, composition, and pacing, creating an experience that reflects the energy and atmosphere of their music. The goal was not to merely document but to create a multisensory narrative where each page feels like a continuation of the sound.

The booklet serves as a physical extension of the band's identity, using visual rhythm and pacing to mirror the structure of their music. Through strategic composition and intentional spacing, readers experience the flow and emotional arc that defines the band's work, transforming the publication into a complete artistic statement.

Design Approach

I developed a structured editorial system using expressive typography, layered imagery, and a consistent grid to establish rhythm across each spread. Scale, hierarchy, and negative space were used to guide the reader while allowing individual layouts to feel dynamic. The grid provided the backbone for consistency while creating room for creative variation throughout the publication.

Every design decision—from typography to image placement—worked together to create a publication that balances readability with visual impact. The type choices reflect the band's aesthetic, with careful attention paid to line length, leading, and kerning to enhance the reading experience and establish a distinct visual voice that resonates with their audience.

Color and imagery were strategically layered to create depth and visual interest across spreads. Photographic sequences and editorial illustrations were arranged to build momentum, guiding the eye through each page while maintaining an intuitive visual hierarchy that feels natural rather than forced, allowing the narrative to unfold organically.

Result

The final booklet demonstrates my ability to build cohesive editorial systems that communicate through both content and form. By combining thoughtful typography with strong visual sequencing, the publication transforms printed pages into an immersive reading experience that extends the band's creative vision into print and elevates their visual presence.

The system remains flexible and scalable, capable of evolving as the band's work develops while maintaining a consistent visual identity. It proves that editorial design, when approached with intentionality and understanding of the subject matter, becomes a powerful tool for storytelling and brand expression that connects deeply with audiences.
