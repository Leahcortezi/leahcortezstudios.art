Billy Joel Tribute Booklet

A sixteen-page tribute booklet exploring Billy Joel's music, with particular attention to Vienna, through archival photography, serif typography, and a cream, charcoal, and burgundy palette.

Deliverables
16-page booklet, typography and layout, colour system
01 — The storyBilly Joel Tribute Booklet
Overview

The project explores Billy Joel's music through its reflective themes and emotional atmosphere, rather than presenting a conventional biography. I wanted the publication to feel considered and personal, using the visual language of vintage print media to support the subject.

The design relies on pacing, image treatment, and typography to create variation across the pages while maintaining a consistent visual identity.

02 — Research & SketchesBilly Joel Tribute Booklet

Listening first

I started with the music and the era around it: album art, archival photographs and the printed look of vintage magazines. From those I pulled a palette of cream, charcoal and burgundy.

Pacing sixteen pages

Thumbnails mapped how the booklet would move from the cover to the back, alternating full-page photographs with pages of text so the rhythm stays steady.

05 — Final designBilly Joel Tribute Booklet

The booklet uses cream pages with photographs treated in black and white or burgundy duotone. Alternating between these treatments creates visual variation while keeping the imagery within a consistent palette.

The cover pairs Billy Joel with Piano Man in script lettering. Inside, headings such as Finding His Voice and The Stranger introduce sections set in narrow serif text columns alongside large photographs. A dedicated pull-quote page breaks up the reading sequence, while a script Billy Joel signature crosses one of the full-page images.

The combination of narrow text columns and larger photographs gives the pages a clear hierarchy. The restrained colors and recurring typography tie the different layouts together, creating the feel of a cohesive printed tribute rather than a collection of unrelated spreads.
