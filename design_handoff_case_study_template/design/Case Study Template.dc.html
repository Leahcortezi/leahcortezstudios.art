Case study · Packaging · 2025

Snack Packaging

A rebrand for Insane Grain, a sorghum-based health snack whose packaging was far quieter than its name. The new pouch is built to grab younger, trend-conscious snackers without losing the health story.

Role
Brand & packaging design
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator
Year
2025
Deliverables
Pouch, pattern, mockups
Overview

Overview

Challenge

The old packaging didn’t carry the energy or boldness the name promised, and it wasn’t speaking to the younger, trend-conscious audience the brand wanted.

Solution

A loud collage system: a halftone mouth, real chilies and inky flame linework, locked into hard red, black and white blocks with tall condensed type.

Outcome

A pouch that sells heat first. The health claims (sorghum, gut health, 47% less fat) get their own black band, so they still read at a glance.

How to use this template

Three blocks are required. Everything else has to earn its place.

  1. 1Start a new copy of this page for each piece.
  2. 2Keep one Hero (split or wide), the Overview and Next.
  3. 3Add Story, Feature, Gallery, Process or Motion blocks in any order. If you can’t fill a block honestly, delete it.
  4. 4Drop images straight onto the slots. Captions are optional, but one line helps.

Voice — keep every page sounding like you

Personal, specific, honest. Made to linger, not to please.

The look is already locked in: type, colour and spacing live inside the blocks. The words are the part you control. Follow the same six rules and word budgets on every page and they’ll all read as one voice.

  1. 1Write as yourself.“I pulled collage portraits…”, not “The designer conducted research…”
  2. 2Name the specific thing.“A halftone mouth and real chilies”, not “bold, eye-catching visuals”.
  3. 3Cut anything generic.If a sentence could sit in anyone’s case study (“research gathers references”), delete it.
  4. 4One idea per sentence.Short beats clever. If it needs two commas, make it two sentences.
  5. 5Honest over impressive.Say what changed, what didn’t work, what you’d push next. No numbers you can’t back up.
  6. 6Same order every time.What it is → why it needed to exist → how you got there → what exists now.
