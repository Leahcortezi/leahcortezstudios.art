About meCorpus Christi, Texas

Hi, I'm Leah.

I grew up in South Texas. My Mexican heritage, my Catholic upbringing and the streets of downtown Corpus Christi show up in almost everything I make.

When I was little I didn't just play pretend. I made the props, designed the costumes and gave every game its own identity. I didn't know it then, but I was already a designer.

From a feeling to a system.

Most projects start with a feeling or a memory I can't put into words yet. Here's how it turns into something you can hold.

  1. (01)

    Research

    I collect imagery, references, textures and words until something feels connected to the idea.

  2. (02)

    Experiment

    Sketches, collage and marker comps, by hand and on screen.

  3. (03)

    Build a language

    Type, image-making, composition and colour turn the idea into a visual system.

  4. (04)

    Refine

    Critique and refinement, round after round, then mockups and print tests.
